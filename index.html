About Yangkhil

Since 2004, Yangkhil Resort has welcomed guests with authentic Bhutanese hospitality and contemporary comfort. Set against panoramic views of Trongsa Dzong, Taa Dzong and the Mangde Chhu valley, Yangkhil offers a peaceful retreat in the heart of Bhutan.

3 star resort with 5+ star staff on side of hill opposite the Trongsa Dhzong and museum. It was quiet (no dogs barking), most rooms have a view, reliable and plentiful hot water, good WiFi (when there is electricity - they rely on the hydro electrical dam and during maintenance periods everyone loses power), super friendly and attentive staff and a great restaurant. If you travel extensively in Bhutan, you quickly learn to appreciate a hotel where everything is truly clean and well maintained. Many hotels seem to struggle with similar plumbing issues, and unpleasant smells can unfortunately be quite common. Bathrooms are often small or in need of attention, and many rooms tend to look more or less the same.

Yangkhil Resort is completely different. Everything is absolutely spotless and beautifully maintained. The bathrooms are spacious, immaculate, and thoughtfully designed, with plenty of room to store your belongings. The furniture and interior design reflect a beautiful, classic style that gives the hotel a warm and elegant atmosphere.

The entire property is surrounded by a beautiful, spacious garden, with an obvious love for detail throughout. The internet connection is also excellent and completely reliable—something that is especially valuable when you need to get some work done while traveling.

The staff are incredibly friendly, helpful, and flexible, making the stay even more enjoyable. We have already started planning our next visit to this beautiful resort and are very much looking forward to returning.
