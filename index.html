About Yangkhil

Since 2004, Yangkhil Resort has welcomed guests with authentic Bhutanese hospitality and contemporary comfort. Set against panoramic views of Trongsa Dzong, Taa Dzong and the Mangde Chhu valley, Yangkhil offers a peaceful retreat in the heart of Bhutan.
